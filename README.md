# Hoop Land Plus

A full NBA offseason for your Hoop Land franchise saves: draft lottery, combine, mock drafts and private workouts,
a live draft, options and extensions, a real salary cap, three weeks of free agency, training camp, Media Day and more.

**Open the app:** https://im-grego.github.io/Hoop-Land-Plus/

1. In Hoop Land, finish the season, advance once to the Pre-Lottery phase and export your save.
2. Open the app (add it to your home screen to use it like an app, even offline) and load the save.
3. Run the offseason, then export the season file at Opening Night.
4. Put the file back into Hoop Land and play the season.

Everything runs on your device. Your save file is never uploaded anywhere.

Hoop Land Plus is a fan-made companion and is not affiliated with Hoop Land or its developer.
